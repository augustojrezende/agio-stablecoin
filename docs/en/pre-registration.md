*English translation of [`docs/pre-registro.md`](../pre-registro.md). The Portuguese original is the binding document: it was registered on 01/11/2026 and is frozen under the tag `congelamento-v2`. If the two texts differ, the Portuguese one prevails. This translation does not change any rule.*

*Registered on 01/11/2026, before any data after 30/10/2026 was collected or analysed. This file must not be edited after the validation starts; changes enter as a dated addendum at the end, with the reason.*

## Why validate out of sample

All thesis tests so far used data from Oct/2020 to Oct/2026 (**discovery sample**). The fiscal hypothesis was fixed before the tests, but the results that emerged later (global fear and incomplete exchange-rate pass-through) appeared while looking at these same data. For them to be asserted, and not just suggested, they need to be confirmed on data that did not yet exist when the hypotheses were written (**validation sample**).

## Samples

| Sample | Period | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discovery | 13/10/2020 to 30/10/2026 | Exploration and test of the original hypothesis. Already analysed. |
| Intermediate validation | 02/11/2026 to 29/10/2027 (about 250 business days) | Check with a stricter criterion (1%). *[Translation note: replaced by a descriptive reading in addendum 2.]* |
| Final validation | 02/11/2026 to 31/10/2028 (about 500 business days) | Decision (5%). |

The abnormal premium uses the median of the previous 60 business days, which may include days from the discovery sample; this uses no result, only the level of the premium.

Command: `agio testar --inicio 2026-11-02 --fim 2027-10-30` (intermediate) and `agio testar --inicio 2026-11-02 --fim 2028-11-01` (final). Each analysis runs **once** on the scheduled date.

## Collection during the validation

- `agio coletar`, `agio coletar-horario` and `agio coletar-controles` run at least once a week (exchange APIs do not guarantee long histories).
- No code changes to the premium measure, the controls or the tests during the validation, except to correct errors, recorded in `decisoes.md` with the reason and without looking at validation results.

## Validation events

- **Scheduled:** generated by the same rule (`scripts/montar_eventos.py`): Copom and Fed decisions, PLDO, PLOA (budget bills), bimonthly fiscal reports. The 2027 and 2028 calendars enter as soon as they are published.
- **Unscheduled:** same criterion as in the discovery (announcement of changes to fiscal rules or extraordinary measures to meet the target; external shock covered by the economic press; Central Bank rule on crypto assets). Each event enters **within 30 days of happening, with a source, and always before any validation analysis**.
- The first round of 2028 is a municipal election and does **not** enter (the rule covers presidential elections).

## Hypotheses and decision rules

All regressions: OLS with Newey-West errors (5 lags), same controls as in the discovery. Event study: window from −1 to +3 business days, permutation with 5,000 draws, seed 42.

| # | Hypothesis | Origin | Test | Confirmed if |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| V1 | Fiscal stress raises the premium | Original thesis hypothesis | Coefficient of `d_juro_bp` in the abnormal-premium regression **and** CAR of domestic events larger than external ones (full list) | Coefficient > 0 with p < α **and** difference > 0 with permutation p < α |
| V2 | Crises raise participation | Behavioural thesis hypothesis | CAR of the abnormal number of trades, all events, against random dates | CAR > placebo mean with permutation p < α |
| V3 | Global fear raises the premium | Discovery (regression and robustness) | Coefficient of `d_vix_ant` | > 0 with p < α |
| V4a | Incomplete same-day exchange-rate pass-through | Discovery (all specifications) | Coefficient of `d_dolar` | < 0 with p < α |
| V4b | Pass-through completes on the following days | New (not tested in the discovery as confirmatory) | Sum of the coefficients of `d_dolar` lagged 1 to 3 days (`regressao_repasse`) | > 0 with p < α |
| V5 | Isolated external shocks reduce the premium | Discovery (exploratory, event study) | CAR of the dollar-adjusted premium, non-overlapping external events | < placebo mean with permutation p < α |

- **α = 1%** in the intermediate validation and **α = 5%** in the final one. The thesis conclusion uses the final one; the intermediate one only allows a confirmation to be anticipated. *[Translation note: replaced by addendum 2, P1.]*
- **Multiple testing:** besides each hypothesis' p-value, the report shows Holm-adjusted p-values for the six hypotheses. The conclusion mentions both.
- **No power:** if an event hypothesis has fewer than 10 events in the validation, the result is declared inconclusive, not negative.
- **Opposite result** (for example, a significant coefficient with the opposite sign) is recorded as such.

## Addendum 1 (01/11/2026, before any validation data)

**Reason.** V4b was written before estimating the pass-through regression. In the discovery sample it came out with the opposite sign to the one predicted: after a rise in the official dollar, the premium keeps falling over the next two days (lags of 1 and 2 days: −0.057 and −0.025; sum of the 3 lags: −0.080, p < 0.001). The total effect reaches about −0.21 p.p. per 1% rise, against −0.13 on the same day only.

**What changes.**
- **V4b stays as written** and will be tested in the validation with the original sign (sum > 0). In the discovery it is not confirmed.
- **V6, derived from the discovery, is added:** "profit-taking". Holders of USDT sell over a few days after a rise in the dollar, and the premium keeps falling.
  - Test: the same sum of lags 1 to 3 in `regressao_repasse`.
  - Confirmed if the sum is < 0 with p < α.
  - V6 emerged in the discovery data; confirmation depends only on the validation.
- The Holm correction now covers 7 hypotheses (V1, V2, V3, V4a, V4b, V5 and V6), and the table shows the decision by the raw p-value (`confirma`) and by the adjusted p-value (`confirma_holm`).

**Situation in the discovery, by the Holm-adjusted p-value.** V3 (0.031) and V4a (< 0.001) hold. V5 falls (0.104) and should therefore be read as weak.

## Addendum 2 (05/11/2026, before any analysis of validation data)

**Reason.** Audits AUD-001 to AUD-003 and an independent review pointed to rules that were missing for the validation. This addendum fixes them before any analysis of data after 30/10/2026. Exposure to new data up to this date is recorded in `congelamento.md` ([English](freeze.md)).

**P1. Sequential strategy (replaces the α = 1% rule in the intermediate validation).**
- **2027, descriptive reading:** `agio testar --inicio 2026-11-02 --fim 2027-10-30` (data until 29/10/2027). It reports estimates and p-values, with no decision. The `confirma` and `confirma_holm` columns of this output do not count as confirmation or refutation.
- **2028, single decision:** `agio testar --inicio 2026-11-02 --fim 2028-11-01` (data until 31/10/2028), α = 5%. It is the only decision of the validation.
- **Why:** deciding in 2027 at 1% and in 2028 at 5% could raise the error per hypothesis to up to 6%. In addition, 2027 will have about 8 domestic and 8 external events, below the minimum of 10.
- `--fim` is exclusive: only days before the given date are included.

**P2. Eligibility and minimum sample** (incorporates the rules approved in AUD-002, `auditorias/AUD-002/AUD-002_DECISOES_V2.md`).
- **V1:** requires at least 10 domestic (fiscal and political) and 10 external events with a valid CAR in the full list. Below that, the whole of V1 is inconclusive, not negative.
- **V2 and V5:** fewer than 10 events with a valid CAR makes the hypothesis inconclusive. Declared now: the discovery had about 2 isolated external events per year, so V5 will probably be inconclusive in 2028.
- **Regressions (V3, V4a, V4b, V6):** require at least 200 days with all variables. Below that, inconclusive.
- **Holm family:** always the seven hypotheses (V1, V2, V3, V4a, V4b, V5, V6). A non-estimable hypothesis keeps its position, with a missing p-value and an inconclusive decision.
- **Days without a control** (missing VIX or real rate) are dropped from the regression, as in the discovery. No value is imputed.

**P3. Confirmation criterion.**
- The decision is `confirma_holm`: predicted sign **and** Holm-adjusted p < α. The raw p-value is reported but does not decide.
- p-values are two-sided, with a sign check, as in the discovery.
- Three possible outcomes: **confirmed**, **not confirmed** (includes the opposite sign, recorded as such) and **inconclusive** (P2).
- V4b and V6 use the same statistic with opposite signs; at most one can be confirmed, and the two do not count as independent evidence.
- V1 uses the full list. The contrast of isolated events does not replace it.

**P4. Calendar, events and data.**
- **Overlap calendar:** days with a published PTAX up to the end of the period (`calendario_ptax` in `scripts/montar_eventos.py`). Never a results file, so that the list can be closed without running the test.
- **2027 and 2028 events:** enter by the same rule of the script (Copom, Fed, PLDO, PLOA, bimonthly reports and unscheduled events with a source, up to 30 days after the fact). `FIM_DADOS` and the years in the script are extended to cover the period. This is the inclusion of data foreseen in the pre-registration, not a change of method, and each update is recorded in `decisoes.md`.
- **List closed before the analysis:** the list for each period goes to GitHub (commit and push) before the `agio testar` command for that period.
- **Collection:** weekly, as in the section "Collection during the validation". Discovery data are not replaced. If a source stops working, this is recorded and no series is replaced without a new addendum.

**P5. Freeze.**
- The version used in the validation is the one under the tag `congelamento-v2`, created on the commit that incorporates the version corrected by the audit and this addendum. The manifest (hashes, dependencies and exposure) is in `congelamento.md`.
- The original discovery version stays under the tag `descoberta-v1` (commit `543fdea`).
- After the freeze, code changes only to correct errors, recorded in `decisoes.md` and without looking at validation results.

**V6 label.** It becomes "Premium keeps falling after a rise in the dollar" ("Ágio segue caindo após alta do dólar"). The test and the criterion do not change. "Profit-taking" remains one of the possible explanations, not part of the hypothesis.
